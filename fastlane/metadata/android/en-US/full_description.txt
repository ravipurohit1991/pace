Pace is a private Android companion for cutting down and stopping smoking. It pairs honest tracking with an optional AI coach that talks you through the hard minutes, proposes something concrete to do, and reaches out before your next scheduled window instead of after.

Your history never leaves the device. There is no account, no analytics, and no telemetry.

TRACKING THAT DOESN'T SHAME YOU

A ceiling, not a quota. One tap logs a moment, with a 12-second undo from the app or the home-screen widget. Minimum spacing, a morning hold, and quiet hours shape the day. A hard day never rewrites history, and a lower ceiling always takes effect tomorrow rather than retroactively.

A COACH THAT NEVER SAYS THE WORD

Naming the substance is itself a trigger, so the coach is built to never say cigarette, smoking, nicotine, craving or quitting. Not once, not even if you say it first. The figures it receives are phrased the same way: moments, waits, clean stretches. If you raise it, it answers the feeling underneath and moves the conversation somewhere else.

Four personalities ship in the box, and the prompt stays fully editable. Replies stream token by token and can be stopped mid-sentence. "Riddle me" pulls a two-minute lateral-thinking puzzle to occupy your head until the moment passes. A voice-only call mode uses the device's own speech recognition and text-to-speech.

THE COACH IS OPT-IN AND BRING-YOUR-OWN-KEY

Pace ships with no AI credentials. The coach does nothing until you enter your own Ollama Cloud API key in settings. If you never enter one, the app never makes a network request and every feature below still works offline.

When the coach is enabled, it sends only the message or voice turn you submit, photos you explicitly attach, and a handful of optional grounding numbers already visible on your screen. Image bytes are sent once and discarded.

WHAT ELSE IS IN THERE

- A home-screen widget for logging without opening the app
- A body-recovery timeline tracking what changes and when
- Badges for the stretches you complete
- A distraction toolkit for the moments that need one
- Progress history by day, week and quarter
- A plan screen where you mark your hardest stretch of the day

PRIVACY

All data lives in a local database on your device. Nothing is uploaded, backed up to a server, or shared. Uninstalling the app removes it. The full privacy policy ships in the repository.
